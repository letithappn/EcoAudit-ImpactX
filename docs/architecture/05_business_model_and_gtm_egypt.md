### 1.1 The Four Distinct Customer Segments
1. **Segment 1: Heavy Industrial Exporters (The Beachhead):**
   - *Profile:* Primary steel manufacturers (Ezz Steel, Suez Steel), aluminum smelters (EgyptAlum), clinker/cement plants (Suez Cement, Arabian Cement), and nitrogenous fertilizer producers (Abu Qir Fertilizers, MOPCO).
   - *Pain Point:* Definitive CBAM entered force on January 1, 2026. Without primary, installation-specific Product Carbon Footprint (PCF) reports capped at $\le 20\%$ default values, exports to the EU common market are penalized with maximum penal tariffs or rejected by European buyers.
2. **Segment 2: EGX-Listed Enterprises & NBFIs (The Core ARR Engine):**
   - *Profile:* Over 240 companies listed on the Egyptian Exchange, plus 1,000+ non-bank financial institutions (commercial leasing, factoring, consumer finance, mortgage finance, and insurance).
   - *Pain Point:* FRA Decrees 107 & 108 mandate annual ESG/TCFD disclosures. FRA Decision 36 of 2026 legally requires NBFIs to report verified Scope 1 & 2 footprints by June 30 and purchase/surrender $\ge 20\%$ CERCs within 90 days, with operating licenses at stake.
3. **Segment 3: Green Finance & Sustainability-Linked Loan Borrowers:**
   - *Profile:* Egyptian mid-caps and large corporates seeking capital expenditure financing from commercial banks (CIB, Banque Misr, QNB Alahli) and Development Finance Institutions (IFC, EBRD, EIB).
   - *Pain Point:* Green credit lines require independently verified quarterly carbon metrics to unlock subsidized interest rates (saving 150 to 300 basis points on loans running into hundreds of millions of EGP).
4. **Segment 4: Supply Chain Industrial SMEs (The Scale Flywheel):**
   - *Profile:* Packaging manufacturers, chemical compounders, logistics fleets, and component suppliers in industrial zones.
   - *Pain Point:* Global and domestic enterprise customers are demanding verified Scope 3 supplier carbon data. SMEs cannot afford international consultants and need an affordable, automated software tool.

## 2. Egyptian Pound (EGP) Pricing Model & Inflation Hedging

Pricing enterprise software in Egypt requires shielding operational margins from foreign exchange devaluation while offering an unbeatable price advantage compared to USD-denominated foreign competitors (e.g., Watershed at $60k–$150k USD / Persefoni at $80k–$250k USD).

### 2.1 The Hybrid SaaS + Advisory Retainer Architecture
Enterprise software adoption in Egypt fails when delivered as pure self-service software. Industrial plants require hand-holding to locate utility meters, digitize legacy paper receipts, and configure organizational boundaries.

EcoAudit AI implements a **hybrid commercial structure**: an annual recurring SaaS software subscription bundled with a mandatory **"Tech-Enabled Advisory" onboarding retainer** delivered during Year 1:

| Commercial Tier | Target Client Profile | Annual SaaS Subscription (EGP) | Year 1 Advisory Retainer (EGP) | Core Functional Entitlements |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1: SME / Supplier** | Mid-tier manufacturing plants (1–2 facilities), local suppliers. | **180,000 – 350,000 EGP** / year | **75,000 EGP** (One-time) | Scope 1 & 2 accounting; energy/water tracking; bilingual OCR (up to 500 invoices/yr); standard PDF ESG reports. |
| **Tier 2: Enterprise / EGX & NBFI** | Publicly listed firms, NBFIs, insurance and leasing institutions. | **600,000 – 1,200,000 EGP** / year | **250,000 EGP** (Year 1 onboarding) | Scope 1, 2, and operational Scope 3; FRA Decrees 107/108 & Decision 36/2026 reporting; Auditor's Room; automated EGX CERC retirement orders. |
| **Tier 3: Industrial Exporter (CBAM)** | Heavy industrial manufacturers (Steel, Cement, Aluminum, Fertilizers). | **1,500,000 – 3,200,000 EGP** / year | **500,000 EGP** (Industrial setup) | Production-line Product Carbon Footprints; recursive Bill of Materials tracking; precursor calculation; quarterly EU CBAM XML export. |
| **Tier 4: Financed Portfolio (Banks)** | Commercial banks, private equity firms, asset managers. | **3,500,000 – 6,000,000 EGP** / year | **1,000,000 EGP** (Custom integration) | PCAF portfolio carbon accounting; financed emissions attribution; green loan covenant verification dashboard; core banking connectors. |

### 2.2 Inflation Hedging & Foreign Exchange Protection
To protect contract revenue against domestic macroeconomic fluctuations:
- **Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) Inflation Indexing:** All multi-year SaaS agreements incorporate a standard statutory escalation clause:
  $$\text{Fee}_{t+1} = \text{Fee}_t \times \left(1 + \min\left(\text{CBE Core Inflation Rate}, 15\%\right)\right)$$
- **Payment Terms:** Annual subscriptions are invoiced **100% upfront** or semi-annually in advance, mitigating mid-contract collection delays.
